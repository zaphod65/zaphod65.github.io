# The Home Team - Slow Bloom

I'll be honest, I listened to this because of the Jeff Loomis guest spot, and it's... fine, I'm not _enthused_.  I think their later material is better - production's a bit slicker, songwriting feels more confident, I think the vocals are a little better.  This one's perfectly enjoyable though, I'm not going to be offended if someone else puts it on.
