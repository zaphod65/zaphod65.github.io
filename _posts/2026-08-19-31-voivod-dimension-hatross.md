# Voivod - Dimension Hatröss

This really is a fascinating release.  I've tried to listen to Voivod before a few times and it's never really clicked, but I finally caught it at the right time... and I really like this.  It's technical, complex, a little dissonant, and I can absolutely hear the influence on a load of modern bands, like Vektor, Cryptic Shift... basically anyone playing highly technical thrash.

I feel like this will _really_ reward repeat listens to get in to the depth of it, and I really want to go through the rest of the band's material now, this was a lot of fun.
