# Voivod - Nothingface

So this is often lauded as Voivod's best work... and I can see why, this is a great reduction in the more frenetic punk/speed influences and leaning more on the proggy, weird aspects, even more so than Dimension Hatröss.

I'm really on a kick with this band at the moment, this really slaps.  Complex, techy, weird... love it.  More to come!
