# Raphael Weinroth-Brown - Pyre

This is some interesting cello-only music.  Cool textures, sounds a bit like Apocalyptica did at the start only more interesting and with what feels like a stronger melodic aspect to me.  I think there's also more use of effects and studio aspects to me, which lends more texture and interest to the sound.  Definitely need to check out more of theis guy's music.
